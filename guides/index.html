Social Skills Rules

Parent guides

Short, practical guides for teaching everyday friendship and learning habits at home or in class. Each one gives you a concrete next step to practice.

  • How to Teach Kids Conversation Skills: Return the Turn
    A simple way to teach children back-and-forth conversation: respond to what was said, ask a related question, and listen. Includes scripts and a 5-minute practice game.
  • What to Do When Your Child Isn't Invited to a Party
    How to help a child who was left out of a birthday party or playdate: what to say, what to avoid, and how to steer toward friendships that go both ways.
  • Teaching Personal Space to Kids Without Shaming Them
    Practical ways to teach children about personal space: what to say, simple practice games, and how to handle different comfort levels.
  • How to Help a Child Join a Game at Recess
    A step-by-step way to teach kids how to join a group game: watch, wait for an opening, ask, and learn the rules. Includes practice ideas for home.
  • Teaching Kids to Accept No From Friends
    How to help children accept "no" from friends without arguing or wearing them down. Scripts, practice ideas, and why respecting no builds better friendships.
  • Following Directions Activities for Kids Ages 8 to 12
    Why bright kids miss what a question asks, plus a four-step routine (read, mark and count, do, check) and practice ideas for careless mistakes.
  • A Family Art Night Game That Builds Conversation Skills
    Use famous paintings to practice turn-taking, listening, and accepting different opinions. A simple screen-light family activity for kids.
